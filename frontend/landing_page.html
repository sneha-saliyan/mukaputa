Everything social, one app

Built for how you already connect

Posts, photos, videos, stories, reels, likes and comments — MukaPuta brings every way you share into a single, familiar feed.

Friends & connections

Find people you know, send a friend request, and build a circle that grows with every new post.

Posts

Share what's on your mind — text, links or moments — right to your friends' feed in seconds.

Photos & videos

Post pictures and clips in full quality, organised in a clean grid on your profile.

Stories

Share a moment that lives for a day. Tap through your friends' stories the moment you open the app.

Reels

Scroll an endless feed of short videos from your friends and discover new ones people are watching.

Likes & comments

React and reply in real time — every like and comment shows up instantly, so conversations keep going.
